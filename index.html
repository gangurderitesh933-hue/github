About Volt Guard AI

Volt Guard AI is an intelligent electricity bill fraud detection assistant built for power distribution companies and utility officers.

Capabilities

  • Analyse electricity consumption trends
  • Compare current usage with historical records
  • Detect billing anomalies and meter irregularities
  • Identify possible meter tampering indicators
  • Estimate fraud risk with explainable AI reasoning
  • Recommend inspection and audit actions
